How to read this manual

Words in blue bold are what you see on the screen — a menu, a button, a field. Numbered circles are steps to do in order. Grey tables headed “What it posts” show the entry the app makes for you; you never type debits and credits on a form.

1What the Finance app is, and who does what

Sand Planet Finance is the company's set of books, kept inside Planet. It is a separate front door — like Trading — with the same sign-in and the same data, so nothing is entered twice.

Opening it

Sign in to Planet as usual, then use the app switcher beside the company name at the top left and choose Finance — or go straight to app.sandplanet.mv/f/.

2Five rules the books keep

These are what make the books something an auditor and MIRA can rely on. The app enforces them; it helps to know them.

  1. Every entry balances. Debits equal credits, in rufiyaa, to the laari. The forms do this for you; a journal cannot be posted until it balances.
  2. Posted is permanent. Nothing posted is edited or deleted. When you change a transaction, the app reverses its old entry and posts a new one; when you void it, the app reverses it. Both stay on record in Books → Journal entries, numbered without gaps (JV-001, JV-002 …).
  3. The books start on 1 January 2026. Nothing can be dated earlier, except the opening balances, which are dated 31 December 2025 — the audited closing figures.
  4. A closed period stays closed. Once a lock date is set, nothing can be posted, changed or voided on or before it, until it is reopened.
  5. The books are in rufiyaa. A dollar transaction keeps its own amount and the rate beside the rufiyaa value. A bank account held in dollars shows dollars in its register and rufiyaa in the statements.

Accounts with a role

Some accounts carry a role the app relies on — GST payable, GST input tax recoverable, the receivable and payable accounts, exchange gain and loss, retained earnings. Rename and re-code them freely, but do not close them: forms and posting rules look for the role.

3.2 Opening balances

The audited balances at 31 December 2025 go in as one entry.

  1. Books → Journal entries → Opening balances. The date is fixed at 31 Dec 2025.
  2. Enter each balance sheet account with its debit or credit, as on the audited statement of financial position: assets as debits; liabilities, share capital and retained earnings as credits.
  3. The foot of the form shows the two totals. When they agree, Post entry.
Check

Reports → Balance sheet “as at” 31 Dec 2025 should now read exactly as the audited statement, and Trial balance should say it is in balance.

3.3 What was still unpaid when the books opened

The opening entry carries receivables and payables as single totals. So that a 2026 payment has something to be set against, list each invoice or bill that was still unpaid on 31 Dec 2025:

  1. Sales → Invoices → + Opening invoice (or Purchases → Bills → + Opening bill).
  2. Customer, the invoice number as issued, its date in 2025, and the amount still unpaid.
  3. Save. It posts no entry — the amount is already inside the opening balances.

When all are listed, Sales → What we are owed ends with a green line: “Accounts receivable per the books … agrees with the invoices above.” Until then it tells you the difference.

3.4 Books settings

Setup → Books settings shows when the books start and holds the lock date (“Books closed up to”). Set it when a month has been checked and reconciled; clear it to reopen. Each change is recorded with who made it.

4.2 Deposit — money received on the spot

+ Deposit is the same form the other way round: Deposit to an account, Received from, and lines saying what the money was — an income account for a cash sale, a liability account for a loan or the owner's money. A sale needs its GST treatment; a line with No GST is not a sale and stays off the GST return.

4.3 Transfer — between the company's own accounts

+ Transfer: From, To, the amount sent, date, reference. When the two accounts are in different currencies, also enter the Amount received — the app shows the rate that implies. A bank's charge for the transfer is a separate expense.

4.4 The register

Each account's register reads like its bank statement: date, number, payee, what it was for, money out, money in and the balance after each line, starting from a balance brought forward. A dollar account's register is in dollars. Use the dates to change the period and ⬇ Excel to take it out.

4.5 Changing or voiding

  • Change: click the line in the register, correct it, Save changes. It keeps its number; the register shows it once.
  • Void: Void…, say why, Void it. It leaves the register; its history stays in the journals.
  • Neither is possible once the transaction is on a finished reconciliation or in a closed period — reopen that first.

The first reconciliation

It starts from nil, and the audited opening balance appears in Money in as a line of its own — tick it. If the bank's own balance on 31 Dec 2025 was different (cheques then outstanding), ask the accountant to split the opening entry so those items are separate lines.

7.3 Letting the bank's file do the ticking

  1. Download the statement from internet banking as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV.
  2. ⬆ Import the bank's statement and choose the file.
  3. The app pairs each statement line with a line in the books of the same amount — first by a cheque or transfer reference the bank quotes, then by the nearest date — and ticks it. It reports how many it found.

The file needs a header row with a Date column and either Debit and Credit columns or one Amount column (with a Dr/Cr marker); title lines above it do not matter. The old .xls format and PDF statements cannot be read — save as .xlsx, or tick by hand.

7.4 What the bank shows that the books don't

Bank charges, interest, a direct credit: these appear in a list headed “On the bank's statement, not in the books”. Click Enter as an expense (or deposit) — the form opens already filled in with the date, amount and description; pick the account (for a charge, 7510 Bank charges) and save. You are brought back, and the line is ticked.

7.5 Finishing, and afterwards

  • Finish is available only when the difference is 0.00.
  • The finished statement then shows the proof: balance per the bank statement, plus deposits not yet credited, less payments not yet presented, equals the balance per the books. ⬇ Excel gives it for the file.
  • A transaction ticked on a finished statement cannot be changed or voided. If one must be, Reopen the reconciliation — only the latest one of an account can be reopened. The next statement starts where this one ended.

9Journal entries

Books → Journal entries is the full record: every entry any form or rule has posted, and the place where the accountant makes entries no form covers.

Making a journal

  1. + New journal. Date and what the entry is for.
  2. Each line: account, debit or credit, and where useful the site, project and party. A line on a dollar account also takes the dollar amount and rate.
  3. Save as draft to come back to it, or Post entry. It posts only if the two sides are equal. A draft can be edited or deleted; a posted entry cannot.

Correcting a posted journal

Open it, Reverse this entry…, give the reason. A mirror entry is posted and both stay on record, each pointing to the other. Then post the correct entry.

10Posting from Planet

Planet already records purchases, payments, payroll, claims and receipts, with their approvals. Setup → Posting from Planet gives each of those events its entry in the books — so they are not keyed again.

10.1 How it works

  • There are twelve rules. Each is off until it is switched on.
  • Preview (one rule) or Preview all shows exactly what would be posted over Planet's history — the count, the total, sample entries with their debits and credits, and anything held back. A preview only reads; nothing is saved.
  • Switch on posts everything the rule covers from the “post from” date, and from then on the books are brought into step every twenty minutes. Post now does it at once.
  • Each event has one entry. A new event is posted; one that changed in Planet has its entry reversed and posted afresh; one that was deleted or voided is reversed. Running it again changes nothing.
  • Switch off stops new postings and leaves the entries. A rule that is off can have its entries taken out — each is reversed, nothing is deleted.

Two questions that come up

Does approving a purchase order post anything? No. A purchase is posted when Planet's cost ledger counts it as incurred — the order signed by the signatory, or the cash purchase approved on a voucher — and its payment when the payment is recorded.
Does a claim post when it is submitted? No — when it is certified and its tax invoice exists.

10.3 Held back, never guessed

When a rule cannot post an event, it leaves it out and says why, by name. The usual reasons and their cure:

A held event is picked up on the next run after it is put right.

10.4 Where each cost head lands

A cost in Planet carries a cost head; the books need an account. The table at the foot of the page shows the account each head posts to — Materials to 5110, Labour to 5130, and so on. Choose another account where the consultant wants it elsewhere. A head with no account holds its costs back until one is chosen.

10.5 Post events dated from

Events before this date are left to the opening balances and to hand entry. It starts as the first day of the books. Dollar invoices and receipts are taken at the company rate, because Planet stores no rate on them.

Do not enter by hand what a rule posts

Once a rule is on, do not also key the same purchase, payment or invoice on a form — it would be in the books twice. Hand entry and Excel import are for what Planet does not hold: January to June 2026, and things that never go through Planet, such as bank charges. The forms help: from the date Planet holds that kind of thing, an expense, bill, invoice or deposit will not save until you tick This is not in Planet.

11Rentals, and phone and utility bills

Operations → Rentals is the register of what the company rents and pays for period after period — offices, staff accommodation, warehouses, yards, vehicles and equipment on hire. Each period's rent is raised by itself and lands on Payables, ready for a payment voucher.

11.1 Adding a rental

  1. + Rental. Say what is rented, its kind, the landlord, where the rent is paid to, and the site that bears the cost.
  2. The rent per period before GST, the currency (MVR or USD), how often it is paid — monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly — and whether it falls due in advance (as the period starts) or in arrears (as it ends). A day of the month can be fixed.
  3. Rental starts, and Ends if the agreement has an end. A last part-period pays for its days only.
  4. Raise dues from: left blank, dues start from today — earlier periods are taken as already paid outside Planet. Give an earlier date to have those periods raised too.
  5. Tick The landlord charges GST if it does. Add any agreed changes of rent with the date each applies from. Attach the agreement. Save rental.

The cost head decides where the rent shows in Planet's cost reports and, through Posting from Planet, in the books; it starts as Rent.

11.2 How dues are raised and paid

  • Every morning Planet raises each period that has come up — by default seven days before its due date. Raise dues now does the same at once; on a rental, Raise … now brings the next period forward.
  • A due appears on Operations → Payables under the rental's reference (RENT-001) with the period it is for. Tick it, Create voucher, and it follows the usual path: submitted, approved by the signatory, paid, settled.
  • The rental's own page lists every due with its status — due, overdue, paid — and the voucher it is on.

11.3 Changes

ToDo
Change the rent or the termsEdit the rental and save. Dues already raised are not touched.
Take back a due raised wronglyCancel… on the due, with the reason. If it is on a voucher, take it off the voucher first. A cancelled period is not raised again by itself; use Raise again once the rental is corrected.
End a rentalSet its end date, or its status to Ended. No more dues are raised; dues already raised stay to be paid or cancelled.

11.4 Phone and utility accounts

Operations → Phone & utility bills keeps every account the company is billed on month after month — each mobile number, internet line, electricity and water meter — so a month's bills are entered on one sheet and a provider's whole batch is paid on one voucher.

  1. + Account: the provider, the kind, the number or meter, the site that bears the cost. Phone and internet bills are taken to include GST; electricity and water are not. Change either on the account.
  2. For a phone, pick the person it is assigned to and the monthly allowance the company bears.
  3. + Several at once takes a pasted list, one account to a line: number, employee number, allowance — for example 7771234, EMP-0060, 500.
  4. A prepaid number has no bill. Set its Billing to Prepaid and give the monthly recharge. On the month's sheet, Fill prepaid recharges puts those amounts in for you. Nothing is ever recovered from a salary for a prepaid number.

11.5 Each month

  1. Enter a month's bills: choose the month. Every account is listed with its allowance and last month's figure. Type each bill's total as printed; leave a row blank if its bill has not come. Save — all go in together, or none.
  2. Bills to pay lists the unpaid bills provider by provider, with the earliest due date. Create voucher puts a provider's bills on one payment voucher (untick any to leave out); ⬇ List gives the numbers and amounts for the provider's portal or the bank. The voucher then goes the usual way: submitted, approved, paid, settled.
  3. A bill entered wrongly is cancelled from the sheet with its reason, and entered again.

11.6 A phone over its allowance

The company bears the allowance; the rest is the person's

When a bill is more than the account's allowance, the provider is still paid in full, the company's cost is the allowance only, and the difference is recovered from the assigned person's salary — it is added to the advances payroll takes for the bill's month, or for the next month not yet drawn up. Salary recoveries lists each one and whether it has been deducted. Once it has been deducted on a locked payroll, the bill can no longer be cancelled.

13GST

The books follow the Maldives Inland Revenue Authority's published rules. The return page is laid out on MIRA's own forms: MIRA 205 (GST Return — General Goods and Services), the Output Tax Statement and the Input Tax Statement, version 25.1.

13.1 The GST treatment of a line

TreatmentUse it forEffect
Standard ratedGoods and services taxed at the general rate (the company setting, 8%).GST is added. On a sale it goes to GST payable; on a purchase with a tax invoice, to input tax.
Zero ratedTaxable at 0% — exports, the essential goods MIRA lists.No GST; reported on the return.
ExemptSupplies MIRA exempts — electricity, water, education, health, financial services, rent of immovable property and others.No GST; reported on the return.
Out of scopeA supply outside the Maldives' GST.No GST; reported on the return.
No GSTNot a supply at all — a loan, the owner's money, a transfer, wages.Not on the return.
MIRA's rules the app applies
  • Input tax needs a valid tax invoice. It is claimed only where the box is ticked, and the app then requires the supplier's TIN and the tax invoice number — MIRA's input tax statement lists both. Otherwise the GST is part of the cost.
  • Twelve months. Input tax cannot be claimed more than 12 months after the period it could first have been claimed in; the page flags such an invoice.
  • Excess input tax is not refunded — it is carried forward against later output tax.
  • Records are kept five years. Entries are permanent and numbered; attach the tax invoice to the transaction.

13.2 The return

  1. Reports → GST return. Choose the taxable period — Last month, Last quarter, or any dates.
  2. Read the warnings at the top first: a sale with no GST treatment, a purchase missing the TIN or invoice number, an invoice over twelve months old. Put them right at the source.
  3. The boxes are those of MIRA 205, rounded to the nearest rufiyaa as the form asks.

14Month-end checklist

  1. Everything entered. Expenses, deposits and transfers for the month; bills received; invoices issued outside Planet.
  2. Posting from Planet. On Setup → Posting from Planet, Preview all: nothing left to post for the rules that are on, and the held-back list cleared or explained.
  3. Reconcile every bank and cash account to its statement; enter the bank's charges and interest; finish each.
  4. What we owe / What we are owed: both agree with the books (the green line). Chase what is overdue.
  5. Accountant's journals: accruals, prepayments, depreciation, corrections.
  6. Review the trial balance, profit and loss and balance sheet. Look into anything unexpected by clicking through to its entries.
  7. GST return for the period if one is due: clear the warnings, download the two statements, file and pay by the 28th; enter the payment to MIRA as an expense to GST payable.
  8. Close the month: Setup → Books settings, set the lock date to the last day of the month.

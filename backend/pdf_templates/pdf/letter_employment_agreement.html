C.  The Employer shall provide the Employee
  1. Free air ticket Colombo–Malé at the beginning of the contract.
  2. Free return air ticket to Colombo in the following cases:
    1. expiration of the contract;
    2. termination of the contract by the Employer without just cause;
    3. if the Employee is unable to continue work due to work-connected disease or injury;
    4. force majeure; and
    5. such other cases where the contract is terminated through no fault of the Employee.
  3. Free food.
  4. Free accommodation with living facilities.
  5. Free emergency medical attention and medicines.
  6. Workmen's compensation benefits for service-connected illness, injury or death, according to the laws of the Republic of Maldives, and war hazard protection.
  7. Company benefits provided to other employees in the same work.
D.  Company Rules and Laws

The Employee shall observe the Employer's company rules and abide by the pertinent laws of the Republic of Maldives.

E.  Remittances

The Employer shall assist the Employee in the regular remittance of the monthly salary and allowances, or any portion of them as the Employee may decide, to the Employee's beneficiaries in Sri Lanka through the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment / the Embassy of Sri Lanka in the Republic of Maldives, if requested.

F.  In the Case of Death

In the case of the death of the Employee during the contract period, the Employer agrees to the repatriation of the remains of the deceased along with the personal effects and savings at the expense of the Employer, and with the payment of compensation.

G.  Compensation
  1. The Employer shall ensure that personal life and accident insurance is provided for the Employee for work-connected illness, injury or death, in accordance with the pertinent laws of the Republic of Maldives.
  2. During the course of the employment, should any accident or sudden death occur to the Employee, the Employer is responsible for the payment of reasonable compensation where the legal system is silent or not active in the country where the Employee is working.
H.  Termination of the Contract by the Employer

The Employer may terminate the contract of the Employee for any of the following just causes:

  1. the closing or cessation of operation of the establishment, or completion of the project;
  2. serious misconduct or wilful disobedience of a lawful order of the Employer or the Employer's representative at work;
  3. gross, habitual or wilful neglect of duties;
  4. fraud, criminal offence or assault by the Employee on the Employer, the Employer's representative or a fellow worker.
I.  Other Grounds of Termination by the Employer

The Employer may also terminate the contract of the Employee in the following circumstances:

  1. installation of labour-saving devices;
  2. redundancy;
  3. retrenchment to reduce or prevent losses; and
  4. if the Employee is found to be suffering from any disease whose continued employment is prohibited by law or is detrimental to the Employee's health and/or that of the Employee's co-workers.

Termination of employment due to the installation of labour-saving devices, redundancy or retrenchment shall entitle the Employee affected to repatriation pay equivalent to one (1) month's pay, or at least one (1) month's pay for every year of service, whichever is higher (a fraction of a year counting as a year), and all other benefits granted to those who may be terminated at the end of the contract through the normal termination process.

J.  Termination of Employment by the Employee
  1. The Employee may terminate the employer–employee relationship before the expiration of the Employment Contract by serving written notice on the Employer at least one (1) month in advance. The Employer upon whom no such notice has been served may hold the Employee liable for damages. The return airfare in this case shall be borne by the Employee.
  2. The Employee may end the relationship without serving any notice on the Employer for any of the following just causes:
    1. serious insult by the Employer or the Employer's representative on the honour and person of the Employee;
    2. inhuman and unbearable treatment accorded to the Employee by the Employer or the Employer's representative;
    3. commission of a crime or offence by the Employer or the Employer's representative against the person of the Employee or any of the immediate members of the Employee's family.
K.  Dispute Settlement Procedures

All disputes arising from the employment contract shall initially be settled amicably through negotiations, with the participation of either a Sri Lankan Embassy representative or a representative of the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment. If attempts at amicable settlement fail, the parties shall have recourse to the laws in effect in the country of employment.

L.  Applicable Laws

Other terms and conditions of employment not covered herein shall be governed by the pertinent laws of the Republic of Maldives, without negating or rendering nugatory other applicable laws of Sri Lanka and international covenants on expatriate employment. In the event of a dispute arising out of the interpretation of this agreement, the English version of the document shall prevail.

(The Contractor and the Subcontractor are referred to individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.)

Recitals
  1. The Contractor has been engaged for {{ project_title }} at {{ site_name }} (the “Project”).
  2. The Contractor wishes to subcontract part of the Project works, and the Subcontractor has agreed to carry out and complete those works (the “Works”) on the terms set out in this Subcontract.

NOW IT IS AGREED as follows:

1.Scope of Work
  1. The Subcontractor shall carry out and complete the works described in Annexure A (the “Works”) at {{ site_name }}, in accordance with this Subcontract, the Contractor’s instructions, and the project drawings and specifications.
  2. All Works shall be performed with the highest skill and care and to the standard required by the Contractor and the Client/Employer.
2.Subcontract Price & Payment
  1. Subcontract Price. The Contractor shall pay the Subcontractor a fixed all-inclusive price for the complete and satisfactory execution of the Works: {{ currency }} {{ price_fmt }} ({{ value_words }}). This price is all-inclusive and covers all labour, materials, equipment, transport, wages, statutory contributions, and any other costs required to complete the Works, unless expressly stated otherwise in this Subcontract.
  2. Advance Payment. An advance of {{ advance_percent }}% of the Subcontract Price shall be paid on signing, to be recovered pro-rata against progress payments.{% if not has_advance %} (No advance applies.){% endif %}
  3. Progress Payments. The Subcontractor may submit a payment request at the end of each month for Works completed to date, supported by a Subcontract Valuation Certificate agreed with the Contractor’s Project Manager.
    4. {% if show_retention %}
  4. Retention. The Contractor shall retain {{ retention_percent }}% from each progress payment. Half of the retention is released on completion and handover; the balance on expiry of the defects liability period.
    5. {% endif %}
  5. Release of Payment. Each approved payment (less advance recovery{% if show_retention %}, retention{% endif %}, and any deductions under this Subcontract) shall be paid within {{ payment_days|default:"the agreed number of" }} working days of the Contractor certifying the payment.
  6. Final Payment. The final payment shall be made after completion of all Works to the Contractor’s satisfaction, correction of defects, and handover.
3.Payment of Workers’ Wages
  1. The Subcontractor is solely responsible for paying the wages of its workers in full and on time.
  2. If a worker of the Subcontractor raises a complaint with the Contractor that their wages have not been paid, and the Subcontractor fails to settle the amount promptly, the Contractor may pay the worker directly and deduct the full amount so paid from any progress payment or other money due to the Subcontractor. The Subcontractor shall not dispute such deduction.
4.Commencement & Completion
  1. Commencement Date: {{ start_date }}.
  2. Completion Date: {{ completion_date }}.
  3. Delays. If the Subcontractor fails to complete on time, the Contractor may deduct liquidated damages of {% if ld_amount %}{{ currency }} {{ ld_amount }}{% else %}the agreed amount{% endif %} for each day of delay{% if ld_cap_percent %}, up to a maximum of {{ ld_cap_percent }}% of the Subcontract Price{% endif %}.
5.Variations to the Works
  1. The Contractor may order additions, deletions or modifications to the Works by written instruction. The Subcontractor shall comply, and the price and time shall be adjusted by agreement based on the Subcontract rates.
  2. The Subcontractor shall not vary the Works without the Contractor’s prior written approval.
6.General Obligations of the Subcontractor
  1. The Subcontractor shall cooperate and coordinate with the Contractor and any other contractors on site.
  2. The Subcontractor is responsible for the safety of its workforce and shall comply with all health, safety and site regulations.
  3. The Subcontractor shall not communicate directly with the Client/Employer on any matter relating to the Works.
  4. The Subcontractor shall not assign or sublet any part of the Works without the Contractor’s prior written consent.
  5. The Subcontractor is responsible for the conduct of its workers and shall remove any worker the Contractor reasonably deems unsuitable.
  6. The Subcontractor shall ensure all its workers hold valid work permits and are legally entitled to work in the Maldives.
7.Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
  1. This Subcontract is governed by the laws of the Republic of Maldives.
  2. Any dispute shall first be settled amicably by negotiation; failing which, it shall be resolved in accordance with Maldivian law.
8.Termination
  1. The Contractor may terminate this Subcontract immediately if the Subcontractor: (a) fails to proceed with the Works with due diligence; (b) breaches any material term of this Subcontract; (c) fails to pay its workers’ wages after a complaint under Clause 3; or (d) becomes bankrupt or insolvent.
  2. On termination, the Contractor may take possession of the site and all materials to complete the Works, and recover any additional cost of completion from the Subcontractor.
9.Notices
  1. All formal notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post or courier to the Contractor, {{ co.legal_name }}, at {{ co.address }}; and to the Subcontractor, {{ sub.name }}, at {{ sub.address|default:"its registered address" }}{% if sub.contact_person %} — {{ sub.contact_person }}{% endif %}{% if sub.phone %}, {{ sub.phone }}{% endif %}.

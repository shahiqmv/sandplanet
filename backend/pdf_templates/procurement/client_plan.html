ⓘ How to read this plan
  • Progress columns — TDS (material / data-sheet approval), Order, Production, Shipment and Delivery — show how far each item has moved. A dash (—) means that step hasn't started yet.
  • Supply — “Sand Planet” means we procure the item; your site code means it is supplied by your side.

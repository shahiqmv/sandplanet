A live forward plan of the key and imported materials for your project. It updates on its own as each item progresses — nothing to download or refresh.

ⓘ How to read this plan
  • Progress columns — TDS (material / data-sheet approval), Order, Production, Shipment and Delivery — show how far each item has moved. A dash (—) means that step hasn't started yet.
  • Supply — “Sand Planet” means we procure the item; your site code means it is supplied by your side.
  • ETA Resort — our current best estimate of arrival at the resort. It is a live projection that shifts as production and shipping firm up — a working estimate, not a fixed commitment.
  • Status — our own early-warning flag from the required date against the projected arrival, so anything slipping shows up early: On track At risk Late.
  • Grouped items — materials ordered in several sizes or variants show as one line; click it to expand every variant.
  • Want a fuller sheet? Use ⬇ Download Excel above for a structured, column-by-column copy you can keep, print or circulate — ⬇ Excel · all variants lists every variant of a grouped item on its own row.
